================================================================================
MODULE FEATURES
================================================================================

MACRO INDICATORS TRACKED:
  1. VIX (^VIX) - Volatility fear gauge
  2. DXY (DX-Y.NYB) - US Dollar Index
  3. US10Y (^TNX) - 10-Year Treasury Yield
  4. OIL (CL=F) - Crude oil futures
  5. GOLD (GC=F) - Gold futures

PER-INDICATOR METRICS (9 fields):
  - value: current price
  - change_1d: 1-day percent change
  - change_5d: 5-day percent change
  - change_20d: 20-day percent change
  - high_20d: 20-day high
  - low_20d: 20-day low
  - percentile_1y: percentile rank vs 1-year range [0, 1]
  - trend: "rising" | "falling" | "flat"
  - interpretation: context-specific assessment

SIGNAL TYPES (4):
  CRISIS   - VIX > 35 (panic conditions)
  RISK_OFF - VIX > 25 + gold rising + yields falling
  RISK_ON  - VIX < 15 + stable yields + weak dollar
  MIXED    - Default for all other combinations

REGIME PATTERNS (6+):
  1. Flight to safety (VIX↑ + gold↑ + yields↓)
  2. Risk-on environment (low VIX + stable yields)
  3. Inflation concerns (oil↑ + elevated yields)
  4. Equity-specific fear (VIX > 25 + soft commodities)
  5. USD strength regime (DXY↑ + oil↓)
  6. Mixed/default patterns with comprehensive analysis
